Your questions answered

Common questions from safety officers and site managers.

What’s included in site management?

Emergency planning, on-site medical support and staff training to make sure your site is prepared for any situation.

How do you ensure quality?

Continuous staff training, regular equipment checks and adherence to industry best practice.

Can you cover large-scale operations and events?

Yes. We have the capacity to manage medical services for sites and events of any size, with quick response times.

What training do you offer for site staff?

CPR, first aid, basic fire fighting and basic life support, tailored to the hazards on your site.

How quickly can you deploy?

Our team is on standby 24/7 and can be deployed rapidly for immediate medical support.

Do you offer custom packages?

Yes. We build packages around your site’s size, shift patterns and risk profile.
