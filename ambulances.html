Medical & trauma emergencies

Heart attacks, strokes, severe injuries and road accidents — our paramedics stabilise first and transport second.

Every second counts. Our crews are trained to manage complex medical and trauma cases on scene, using the same equipment you’d find in a resuscitation room. We treat with precision and compassion, and we keep the people waiting at home informed.

  • Cardiac and stroke callsECG monitoring, defibrillation and time-critical transport to the right facility.
  • Road traffic collisionsScene safety, spinal care and trauma management on the N4, N12 and local roads.
  • Medical emergencies at homeBreathing difficulty, diabetic emergencies, seizures, falls and more.

What happens when you call

The first minutes decide the outcome, so here’s exactly what to expect.

  1. Your call is answeredA trained call-taker answers 061 9960 911 and asks where you are and what happened.
  2. A crew is dispatchedThe nearest ALS crew is sent while you’re still on the line. Stay on the phone if you can.
  3. Care starts on sceneParamedics assess, stabilise and begin treatment before moving the patient.
  4. Safe handoverWe transport to the most suitable hospital and update your family along the way.

Interfacility transfers

Safe, monitored transport between hospitals when a patient needs care their current facility can’t provide.

We coordinate directly with the sending and receiving medical teams so treatment continues without a gap. Our ambulances carry advanced life support systems and our crews are trained for a wide range of conditions — so families can focus on recovery, not logistics.

  • Hospital-to-hospitalIncluding ICU and specialist referrals across Mpumalanga and Gauteng.
  • Booked or same-dayScheduled transfers and urgent moves, arranged with the ward.
