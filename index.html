What Advanced Life Support means for your family

A basic ambulance gets a patient to hospital. An ALS crew starts hospital-level treatment where they find you.

  • Airway and breathingAdvanced airway management and ventilation on scene and in transit.
  • Heart monitoringContinuous cardiac monitoring and defibrillation for heart attacks and arrhythmias.
  • Medication and IV accessPain relief, fluids and emergency drugs given before you reach casualty.
  • You stay informedWe keep families updated through the transfer — something our clients mention again and again.
