Today’s footsteps, tomorrow’s Legacy.

A registered Advanced Life Support ambulance service, accredited by the Mpumalanga Department of Health. Established in 2016.

From one ALS service to a complete emergency partner

Legacy began in 2016 as an Advanced Life Support emergency service in Emalahleni.

As our clients’ needs grew, so did we. Today Legacy covers every part of pre-hospital care: emergency response, transfers, event and site medical, training, equipment sales and specialist VIP transfers.

We believe in a hands-on approach to every project, in teamwork as the foundation of success, and in giving each client specialist, individual attention.

Our mission

To save lives, reduce suffering and enhance quality of life through accessible, responsive patient care and transport — delivered by a unified team of highly qualified professionals, with integrity at all times.

Our vision

To be the preferred service provider for every client through excellence and efficiency, and to build collaborative relationships with the public, healthcare facilities, other ambulance services and community organisations.

What we stand for

Nine values that shape how we treat patients, clients and each other.

EmpathyWe understand and meet our patients’ healthcare and emotional needs.
ResilienceWe put the same passion and energy into every patient’s fight.
